# BiasharaAI Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 4, 2026

BiasharaAI is an offline-first business app for small and medium businesses. It helps owners record sales, stock, customers, debts, expenses, receipts, service jobs, ledger entries, and business notes.

## Data Stored On The Device

By default, business data is stored locally on the device:

- products, services, stock, prices, and suppliers
- sales, returns, receipts, expenses, cash counts, and ledger records
- customer names, phone numbers, debts, and notes entered by the owner
- chat history, AI feedback, business profile, and app settings
- scanned text from receipts, proof of payment, or pasted payment messages

BiasharaAI excludes its local business database and secret preferences from Android cloud backup.

## Permissions

BiasharaAI may request:

- Camera: barcode scanning, receipt scanning, proof capture, and product photos.
- Microphone: optional voice input and speech transcription.
- Bluetooth: optional thermal printer connections.
- Notifications: local reminders and business alerts.
- Network access: model downloads, optional cloud AI, optional enterprise upload, and optional payment-provider integrations.

Permissions are used only when the owner uses the related feature.

## Optional Cloud AI And Remote Uploads

Local AI remains the default. Optional cloud AI and enterprise uploads are off until configured by the owner. If enabled, BiasharaAI can send the selected prompt, app context, business profile, or analytics payload to the owner-configured gateway or enterprise endpoint. Provider API keys should stay on that gateway, not inside the Android app.

## Payments

BiasharaAI records and reconciles payments for business bookkeeping. If payment-provider integrations are added, payment processing is handled by the selected licensed provider under that provider's terms.

## Data Control

The owner controls local business data. Export reports from the app before transferring or replacing a device. To remove local app data, use Android system settings to clear BiasharaAI storage or uninstall the app.

## AI Reports

Users can report AI answer issues from the chat message menu or Settings. Reports may include app version, message ID, and the selected answer excerpt.
